[news-image-20261125](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261125) bounty on
[make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The image

A newspaper front page, "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — WEDNESDAY 25 NOVEMBER
2026", drawn entirely in code. It covers the day's news as reported by the
early hours of 25 November 2026 (day-so-far coverage — the image was built
while the day was still young, and says so on its face):

- **Space — first Pakistani in orbit.** China's Shenzhou-24 lifts off for
  the Tiangong station, carrying Muhammad Zeeshan Ali — the first Pakistani,
  and the first foreign astronaut, aboard it.
- **Tech & trade — Huawei convicted.** A US jury finds the Chinese telecom
  giant guilty of conspiring to steal trade secrets, and of bank fraud.
- **Markets — Nvidia's wild ride.** Shares fall about 6% a day after
  surging 31.5% on Jensen Huang's return as CEO; the reinstated chief
  apologises to staff and says he is in therapy.
- **Middle East — Riyadh tower hit again.** A Houthi attack strikes the
  PIF skyscraper a second time in a week, injuring 11; Iran says tankers
  hit mines near the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Gaza — Hamas security chief killed.** An Israeli airstrike in Gaza City
  kills Naeem Abu Nahel; medics say at least seven Palestinians were
  killed.
- **Women, Nov 25 — end violence against women.** The International Day
  for the Elimination of Violence against Women; the UN says five women an
  hour are killed by a relative.
- **Also today.** CIA and the ODNI hit by a cyber breach of unclassified
  data; Trump pardons turkeys Freedom & Feathers at the White House;
  Gukesh holds Sindarov in the World Chess Championship.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the shipped `entry.png`
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`), byte for
byte, so both grader runs write identical output. No network is used.
